Privacy Policy for Boring (无聊AI)

App name: Boring (无聊AI)  |  Package: org.eu.huling.ai.boring  |  Effective date: October 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Boring does not collect, store, or upload any personal data. The app has no account system; it does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, or any other user-identifying information, and it does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. All browsing history and favorites are kept only on your device and never leave it.

2. Why We Access the Internet

The app requires internet access solely for the following purposes:

  • Downloading card images from public image content sources (free image services such as Lorem Picsum);
  • Detecting network connectivity and handling unavailable networks gracefully.

These requests carry only the necessary image address parameters and contain no personally identifiable information. Ownership and licensing of image content is provided by the respective public image sources.

3. Local Storage

  • Browsing history (up to 500 entries) and favorites (up to 200): stored on your device only;
  • Image cache (up to 50 MB): used to speed up display; can be cleared at any time;
  • On Android 9 and earlier, saving images to the gallery requires storage permission; on Android 10 and later, the system media library permission is used.

4. Data Sharing

The app shares no user data with any third party. The only data leaving your device is anonymous image download requests to public image sources, which contain no personally identifiable information.

5. Children's Information

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
